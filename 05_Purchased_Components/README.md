# Reference components - no supplier CAD

All STEP files in this folder were generated locally as simplified packaging geometry. The folder name follows the brief; it does **not** mean these are purchased, approved, licensed supplier models or exact representations of Shimano/SRAM products.

| Model | Nominal representation | Required replacement/decision |
|---|---|---|
| Front_Wheel | 680 OD x25 tire torus, generic rim/spokes, 100 mm hub | Tire/rim combination, axle and hub drawing |
| Rear_Wheel | Same tire envelope, 130 mm hub | Hub/freehub, axle, dish, wheel and cassette compatibility |
| Cockpit | 100 mm stem centerline; 420 mm bar width; segmented drops | Actual bar/stem, clamp diameters, reach/drop, controls |
| Seatpost_Saddle | 26.4 mm post envelope and simple oval saddle | Post/frame fit, clamp/rail detail, rider-fit setting |
| Crankset | 172.5 cranks, 210 mm ring OD envelope, spindle and block pedals | BB standard, chainline, Q-factor, actual chainring teeth and pedals |
| Rear_Sprocket | 70 mm OD ring at Y=-45 | Actual cassette stack and freehub interfaces |
| Chain_Path | Two straight nominal tangent strands only | Chain pitch/width, wrap, slack, shifts and derailleur |

Illustrative tooth counts are 50 and 17 for a future gear-mate demonstration. The displayed circles do not contain tooth geometry and do not verify chain engagement. Brakes, shifters, derailleurs, cables and fasteners are intentionally not modeled at this concept stage and remain open design work.

Record manufacturer, exact part number, source URL, revision, units, license and verified interfaces when replacing any placeholder.
